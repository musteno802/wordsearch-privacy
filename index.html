Privacy Policy — Word Search

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Developer: KZops

App: Word Search (Android, package com.musteno802.wordsearch), version 0.3.2 and later unless updated here

Contact: kingsoptions@gmail.com

Summary

Word Search does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share any personal or non-personal data. Everything the game remembers stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

The game saves your progress in a file on your device only: coins, daily streak and best streak, settings, unlocked levels/skins, words you have found, and an unfinished puzzle. This file is never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email, age, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.
  • No advertising and no advertising ID in this version (0.3.2). If ads are added in a later update, this policy will be updated before that update is released.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or tracking tools, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No in-app purchases are active. If purchases are added later, Google Play handles payment and this policy will be updated before that release.
  • The release version does not request internet access.

Device features

  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback if enabled. Nothing leaves the device.
  • Text-to-speech: if "Say words aloud" is on, the device's built-in voice speaks words. Handled by your device's system speech service; the game sends no data.
  • Puzzle codes: "Make your own" creates a text code from your words. Nothing is uploaded; the code only goes somewhere if you choose to copy and send it yourself. Please don't put personal information in custom puzzles.

Children's privacy

Word Search is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app collects no data, there is no data from children to collect, use, disclose, or delete. This is intended to be consistent with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU GDPR, and Google Play's Families Policies. Parents or guardians with questions can contact us at kingsoptions@gmail.com.

Your rights

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete. You control all game data on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if ads or purchases are ever added), we will update this page and the effective date, and describe the change in the app's "What's new" notes before it takes effect.
